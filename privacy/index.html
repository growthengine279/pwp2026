Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This website is run by AOK Perpetual Pty Ltd (ABN 30 690 065 279), trading as Perpetual Wealth Partners. In this policy, "we" and "us" means that business. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and what you can do about it. We aim to handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Your calculator answers: your age, home value, mortgage balance, monthly repayments and household income.
  • Your questionnaire answers, such as when you hope to buy and what you want from a call.
  • Your booking details, if you book a strategy session.
  • Website activity, such as the pages you visit and the ad or link that brought you here.

Why we collect it

  • To show you and email you your calculator results.
  • To book, confirm and remind you about a strategy session.
  • To contact you by phone, SMS or email about your enquiry.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

If you do not give us this information, we may not be able to send you your results or book a session.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with service providers who help us run this business, including:

  • GoHighLevel, for our customer records, emails, SMS and bookings.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website.
  • Google, for Google Tag Manager and website fonts.
  • Meta, for the Meta Pixel and advertising on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Our marketing agency, which helps us run our ads and this website.

Some of these providers may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses cookies and similar tools, including the Meta Pixel and Google Tag Manager. They help us see which ads and pages work, and may be used to show you our ads on other websites and apps. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

Marketing messages

You can opt out of our emails by using the unsubscribe link, and out of our SMS by replying STOP.

Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only for as long as we need it for these purposes, or as the law requires.

General information only

The calculator gives general information only. It does not take your personal circumstances into account and it is not financial advice.

Access, corrections and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by contacting us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
